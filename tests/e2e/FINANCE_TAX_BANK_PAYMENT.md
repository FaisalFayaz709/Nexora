3. M10-SUPPLIER-INVOICE-VARIANCE-BLOCKS-APPROVAL
4. M10-PAYMENT-IDEMPOTENCY-HASHES-CANONICAL-REQUEST-BODY
5. M10-PAYMENT-PARTIAL-FULL-BALANCE-RECONCILIATION
6. M10-TAX-CALCULATION-STORES-AUDITABLE-SNAPSHOT
7. M10-BANK-STATEMENT-IMPORT-CREATES-RECONCILIATION
8. M10-BANK-RECONCILIATION-CLOSE-IS-IMMUTABLE
9. M10-PAYMENT-VOUCHER-POSTS-JOURNAL-AND-CHEQUE-REGISTER
10. M10-FINANCE-REPORTS-CANNOT-BYPASS-TENANT-RBAC-SCOPE
